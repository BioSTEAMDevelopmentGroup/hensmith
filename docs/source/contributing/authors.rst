Authors & acknowledgements
==========================

hensmith was originally created and developed by Sarang S. Bhagwat as the
heat exchanger network synthesis facility of BioSTEAM
(``biosteam.facilities.hxn``, 2020), and is developed and maintained within
the `BioSTEAM Development Group <https://github.com/BioSTEAMDevelopmentGroup/hensmith>`__.
Sarang continues to lead hensmith developments and maintains the repository.

Here is a growing list of core contributors who have committed significant
resources, expertise, and other impactful efforts:

* Sarang S. Bhagwat (creator, lead developer and maintainer)
* Yoel Cortes-Peña (co-developed the ``HeatExchangerNetwork`` facility)

hensmith is released under the University of Illinois/NCSA open-source
license; see ``LICENSE.txt`` in the repository.
